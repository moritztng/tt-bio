--- 4. THE FLOOR UNDER EACH SCHEDULE ---
  C64 adds its two stages (the tail reduces over all D of x, so it cannot join a channel
  pass); R has one pipelined stage because the tail folds into the row block.
  C64 at the machine roof : 2.649 ms   (byte-bound: 8Z is 2.617 ms of traffic against 2.223 ms of math)
  R   at the machine roof : 2.223 ms   (compute-bound: 3Z is 0.981 ms of traffic, 2.27x under the math)
  -> the schedule decides WHICH SIDE BINDS. R is the true compulsory floor: 2.223 ms.

  the same arithmetic at the production call sites' own measured rates (54.97 / 42.20 / 43.34 TFLOP/s): 5.714 ms
  at those rates BOTH schedules are compute-bound, so 8Z -> 3Z is worth 0.000 ms today.
  the byte saving starts to pay only once the arithmetic drops under 8Z's 2.617 ms, i.e. above a class rate of 105.0 TFLOP/s (2.49x today's contraction rate).

--- 5. THE COMPULSORY TRANSPOSE, and why it is sub-tile ---
  The projections are pair-local and contract over d; the contraction is channel-local and
  contracts over k. Each output x[i,j,:] needs a[i,:,:] and b[j,:,:], so a core holding
  channels must hear from every core holding pairs: a full all-to-all, 3Z of it (a, b, x).
  payload = 3Z = 402.7 MB.

  Why it cannot be done tile-granular ACROSS cores: a matmul tile op contracts the inner
  tile axis, so for x[i,j,d] = sum_k a[i,k,d] b[j,k,d] the Hadamard axis d must sit OUTSIDE
  the tile. Keeping d inside the tile turns the contraction into a broadcast-multiply-
  accumulate at 1024 lanes per instruction instead of 32768 MACs, i.e. 32x less
  arithmetic per issue: the contraction would go 0.556 -> 17.8 ms. Dead.
  So d is inside the tile for the projections and outside it for the contraction, and the
  granule of the exchange is one 32-element column = 64 B. That is exactly the
  66.1 GB/s the channel move measures, and it is COMPULSORY at the axis level.

  measured move rates: DRAM path   66.1 GB/s    L1->L1 at C=32  108.9 GB/s
  3Z at the L1->L1 rate                :  3.696 ms
  shipped today (fwd 1.534 + back 1.017 ms, DIFFERENT PARTS):  2.551 ms = 158 GB/s

  What is NOT compulsory is paying 64 B on the NOC. Block the exchange into 32x32x32 cubes
  (65.5 kB = 32 whole tiles): move the 32 tiles core-to-core at 2048 B each and
  rotate the cube inside the receiving core's own L1, where a 64 B granule is a local SRAM
  read and not a NOC packet. The inter-core traffic is then tile-granular and the sub-tile
  work never leaves a core.

--- 7. TWO ROBUSTNESS CHECKS ON THE CONCLUSION ---
  (a) 3Z does not depend on precision. 2Z needs a, b and the gate all resident; even at
      bfp8_b that is 3 x 71.30 = 213.9 MB against the 134.1 MB measured ceiling.
      Holding two role tensors at bfp8_b is 142.6 MB, still over. 3Z stands at every dtype.

  (b) the kernel-rate deficit is NOT an arithmetic-throughput problem, so raising the math
      fidelity does not collect it. HiFi2 halves the FPU passes per bf16 tile product, and
      `TT_BIO_TRUNK_MATH_FIDELITY` already exposes it; the knowledgebase records the trunk
      measurement as 6 % and a broken fp32_dest_acc, and bf4 as 0 % plus accuracy loss.
      A matmul at 34 % of roof is not waiting on FPU passes, which is
      consistent with row 2 above and with fixterm's per-output-tile decomposition.
