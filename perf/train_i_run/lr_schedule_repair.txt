LR SCHEDULE REPAIR -- 2026-09-19, train-i-run, qb2 pair (2,3)
============================================================

WHAT WAS WRONG
  params.yaml optimiser: carries six keys. RECIPE (tt_bio/train/abodybuilder3_step.py) carried
  lr and weight_decay and dropped T_0: 50, T_mult: 1, eta_min: 0 -- the
  CosineAnnealingWarmRestarts that src/abodybuilder3/lightning_module.py:144 builds on the
  RAdam branch at "interval": "epoch". Nothing wrapped the optimizer's lr, so the run held a
  constant 5e-4 from step 1.

THE FIX
  abb3_run.CosineRestartsByStep installs torch's own CosineAnnealingWarmRestarts (upstream's
  class, not a re-derivation) and samples it at the fractional epoch (global_step - 1)/132.
  Fractional rather than upstream's 50-value per-epoch staircase, because it makes the lr a
  pure function of the global step: a resume lands where the step index says and no scheduler
  state has to survive in the checkpoint. With 9-47 watchdog resets expected over this run, a
  call-counted scheduler would restart at the top of the cosine every time.
  132 steps/epoch = 8,395 structures at global batch 64 with the short final batch kept, and
  132 x 1466 = 193,512, upstream's released global_step.
