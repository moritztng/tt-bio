# The interaction the ceiling row could not see: target size and design COUNT are orthogonal
# axes, and mgx-design-ceiling walked size at one design per job. A 1536-residue target that
# folds at 1 design may refuse at 8, because the batch axis multiplies the diffusion working
# set and nothing else.
--model pxdesign --target-res 1536 --designs 8 --steps 400 --host-threads 2 --timeout 28800 --tag ceilscale
--model boltzgen --target-res 1536 --designs 8 --bg-steps design --host-threads 2 --timeout 28800 --tag ceilscale
--model rfd3 --target-res 1536 --designs 2 --steps 100 --batch_size 2 --contig A1-1008,B1-428,100 --host-threads 2 --timeout 28800 --tag ceilscale
# pxdesign's known wall is on TOKENS (1664, one 1.42 GB pair-transition buffer), while its
# recorded ceiling is in TARGET residues. A 1536 target with the 80-residue binder above is
# 1616 tokens, under it; the platform lets a user ask for a 200-residue binder, which is not.
--model pxdesign --target-res 1536 --designs 1 --steps 400 --binder 160 --host-threads 2 --timeout 21600 --tag binderaxis
