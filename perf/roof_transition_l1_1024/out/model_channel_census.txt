So the raise speaks for boltz2, openfold3 and rfd3 only. At 256 and above the guard
declines it, which means TT_BIO_TRANSITION_L1_ROWS=0 is a NO-OP for esmfold2,
protenix-v2 and OpenDDE: the branch is not taken at their channel either way. The
OpenDDE override in the comment above _BH_TRANSITION_L1_ROWS_MAX_C predates that guard.
