  projection      shear (fslice)   : +0.0589 A at FSC 0.143, n=12, CI [+0.0517, +0.0661]
  backprojection  shear adjoint    : NEVER MEASURED in Angstroms (no 0.143 or Angstrom figure in its state doc)
backprojection precision work answered accumulation, not interpolant: bf16 grows ~sqrt(n) to 1.12e-2 rel L2
  at 84 contributions, so fp32 accumulation is a requirement. That part is settled.
evidence the bproj kernel is shear-based: a DRAM source cannot express the shear (needs L1-resident slice)
