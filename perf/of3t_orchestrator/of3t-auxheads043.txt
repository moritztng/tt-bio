## Why this row exists

D94, pass 176. Both upstream modules backing `aux_heads` differ between 0.4.3 — the revision our
port targets, and the revision matching our `of3-p2-155k` preview2 checkpoint — and 0.5.0, which
the campaign's reference bundle was built on:

- **`prediction_heads.py`**: 0.4.3 wraps `embed_zij` (the si->zij projection and the distance-bin
  embedding) in `autocast("cuda", float32)` and casts back; **0.5.0 removes it**. And 0.5.0
  **adds** `autocast("cuda", pairformer_dtype=float32)` around the confidence pairformer stack
  where 0.4.3 had none. **Two precision changes in opposite directions.**
- **`head_modules.py`**: 0.5.0 feeds the confidence head's pairformer embedding `single_mask=
  token_mask` where 0.4.3 passed `single_mask=repr_x_mask`, the representative-atom mask. On
  `batch_step003` those are identical (56 = 56, 0 differing tokens) so it may contribute nothing
  **here**, but that was measured on one datapoint and is not a general result.

The `no_bin` 15 -> 39 in that diff is a **docstring** catching up to a config that already read 39
in both revisions. It is nothing. Do not chase it.

So `aux_heads`' A18 forward failure at **3.6515e-01** may be measuring the revision, not our port.
**The scope may be unmeasured rather than broken**, and 2.8431 % of the model turns on which.
