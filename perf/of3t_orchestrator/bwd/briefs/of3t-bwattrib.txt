   name, shape, dtype, layout, count, total. Not a total and not a mean: the distribution, because

   verb classes carrying it). Those are different defects with different fixes.
2. CAUSE: the suspects are already named and cheap to separate. `Tensor.evict` moving activations

   count you actually observed.
