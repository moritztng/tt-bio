**J1 — the fused LayerNorm backward, adapted from `ttml::metal::layernorm_bw`. The biggest kernel
job on the list, worth at least 46.4 s and plausibly 150 s+.** LayerNorm backward is **1,296 of
2,473 tape nodes, 52.4 %** — the largest single class in the backward by node count, an order of
magnitude larger than triangle attention.

The cost is not the norm maths, it is `_sum_leading`. On an fp32 input it runs `_tree_sum`, a
halving tree of `ttnn.slice` + `ttnn.add_` + `ttnn.deallocate` at ~5 verbs per level and ~13 levels
on a pair-track `[147456, 128]` flattening, then a second tree: **~96 verbs per `_sum_leading`,
~208 per pair-track node**. On bf16 it falls through to a single `ttnn.sum` at ~24 verbs.
`layernorm_bw` computes the per-row dgamma/dbeta partials in the kernel and leaves one reduction,
so the replacement is ~7 verbs per node.

**Three things the survey already established — carry them, do not rediscover them:**

- **`mean` and `rstd` are not a blocker.** `ttnn.layer_norm` returns neither, which is what ruled
  out `moreh_layer_norm_backward`, but our own backward already recomputes both. Pass them in.
- **Precision is a gate to check, not a blocker.** `layernorm_bw.cpp:29` reduces with `ttnn::sum`
  at `precise()`. `_tree_sum`'s docstring measures `ttnn.sum` at **8.3e-4** relative L2 from
  float64 against the tree's **7.7e-8** (`perf/bcx_reduce`). 8.3e-4 is 60x under this campaign's
  5.0e-02 per-tensor bar. If it reads outside anyway, keep our tree on the kernel's partials and
  bank most of the win rather than abandoning the job.
- **Do not grade this job on the four LayerNorm affine leaves.** `of3t-bwdaccum` found them holding
  92.68 % of the error mass while their own backward is correct to 1.4e-02 on the operands handed
  to them: the error is injected upstream by AttentionPairBias. This job will look guilty for
  someone else's reason if you score it there.

**Also settle the arithmetic the survey could only bracket.** The verb bracket is 31,104 to 269,568
and the upper branch is impossible against a measured 168,922 — so most LayerNorm backwards must be
taking the bf16 fall-through today and the fp32 ones are disproportionately expensive. Measure
which, because it decides whether this job is worth 46.4 s or 150 s+.

**Wiring.** You do not need a C++ bridge. `autograd._tape(out_value, parents, make_fn, reads)`
wraps any forward value with any hand-written VJP, and `autograd.triangle_attention`'s `value=`
argument already pairs a shipped fused forward with an authored backward in one tape node
(`taped_ttnn.py:862`). It is a one-off; if you need the same seam on the LayerNorm op, generalise
it there and say so — that generalisation serves J2 and J3 too, so it is shared work and belongs
in your diff only if you are first to need it.
