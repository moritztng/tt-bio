**`zeros` is the largest single verb in OF3T's real backward and it is 424x off its own roof.**

`of3t-bwattrib` established that `exact_training` is 95.2 % of the measured backward, so the
REAL backward — the device compute — is **33.63 s**, not 705.78 s. Against that denominator:

    zeros [384, 1, 384, 128] BFLOAT16 TILE
      324 calls, 11.80 s self, 35.1 % of the 33.63 s backward
      36.4 ms a call at 1.04 GB/s

That shape is **37.75 MB**, the pair track. A write-only fill at the **440 GB/s** bandwidth roof
that binds this workload is **0.086 ms**. It is running at 36.4 ms. **424x.**

Under the old denominator this verb read 1.23 s and 0.17 % — the smallest of four suspects, not
worth anyone's time. That is the whole lesson of R212 in one row: a step that is 95 % measurement
instrument ranks every lever by its share of the instrument.

1. **CONFIRM** the share on your own arm before you touch anything, with the exactness state
   named — `exact_training(False)`, per the sprint's grading convention, because with it ON this
   verb is invisible. A number without that setting written down is not comparable.

   out what those 324 calls are: gradient accumulators being allocated and zero-filled per node
   rather than reused? A `zeros` where a `zeros_like` on an existing buffer would do? An
   allocation inside a loop that could be hoisted? Name it with a break control that MOVES the
   number — a cause you cannot make worse on purpose is not established.

5. **SPEED.** Seconds off the REAL backward, arms interleaved, DURING-sampled AICLK, board class
