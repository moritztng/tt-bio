**J3 — fuse the triangle-attention backward, adapted from `ttml::metal::sdpa_bw`.**

**Start from the honest frame, because the campaign's own shorthand gets it wrong: we are not
missing a bias gradient.** `autograd.triangle_attention`'s backward emits `dbias` today
(`autograd.py:1975-2010`), as `ttnn.sum(ds, dim=0, keepdim=True)` on the broadcast path and
`ttnn.clone(ds)` per trunk otherwise — the two cases distinguished because the atom transformer's
local windows carry a different bias per trunk and summing there would average the windows
together. It is correct and it is gradient-checked (`perf/hallgrad/gradcheck.py --cases
triatt_chunked`, which differences two chunkings rather than trusting one). **What it is not is
fused.** The job is to fuse a term we already compute, not to add one.

**What the survey settled, and you inherit rather than rediscover:**

1. **`grad_bias` is one `pack_tile` from a register the kernel already holds.** The forward's
   composite is `S = scale·QK^T + bias`, so `dL/dbias = dL/dS = P ⊙ (dP - u)` — which is
   `grad_reg` in `compute_grad_scores` (`sdpa_bw/device/kernels/compute/sdpa_bw_compute_utils.hpp`)
   **one instruction before** its final `mul_unary_tile(grad_reg, scaler_bits)`, and `dL/d(QK^T)`
   is the same register after it, which is what dK already uses. Cost in the compute kernel: one
   extra `pack_tile` into a new CB. That is the whole of the maths.
2. **The job is the REDUCTION, not the gradient.** Triangle attention's bias is `[1, H, N, N]`
   broadcast over B, and B is the pair tensor's own first index — at crop 384, 384 independent
   attention problems sharing one bias. So `grad_bias[h,j,k] = Σ_i dS[i,h,j,k]`, and dS at full
   extent is **452.98 MB per call** against 1.18 MB for the reduced answer. Writing dS out and
   reducing it in ttnn is ~0.9 GB of round trip per call = **2.1 s at the 440 GB/s roof**, against
   a whole-op arithmetic cost of 72.5 GFLOP ≈ **1.7 ms** at the 43 TFLOP/s DRAM-bound cap.
   **Materialising dS is three orders of magnitude the wrong answer** — if you find yourself doing
   it, stop.
3. **So: accumulate grad_bias in L1 across i inside the kernel and write it once.** The current KV
   work split assigns contiguous `global_row_idx` and rows are i-major (`row = i·Ht + k_tile`), so
   one core sees one i and many k tiles — precisely the wrong axis. **Strided assignment** (a core
   owns one k tile and strides by `Ht` across i) puts the whole i sum inside one core. The L1
   accumulator is `Ht` tiles per head: at crop 384, 12 tiles × 2 KB = **24 KB** against 1.5 MB per
   core. dK and dV stay correct under the re-split because both are per-i anyway; only the loop
   order changes.
4. **Their mask is binary, ours is an additive learned bias.** `apply_mask_on_reg` turns a 1/0 mask
   into 0/−inf. A fourth `AttentionMaskType::AdditiveBias` that skips the transform and just adds
   is a few lines, **needed symmetrically in `sdpa_fw` and `sdpa_bw`**.
5. **Their forward is not our forward.** `sdpa_bw` needs `intermediates` — the per-row logsumexp
   tile, fp32, from ttml's `sdpa_fw`. Our production forward is the stock ttnn SDPA transcription
   and returns the output only. Flash attention computes the running max and sum internally, so
   emitting them is a writer change to `tt_bio/kernels/triatt_sdpa/`, not new arithmetic.

**THE TRAP, already root-caused, and it has already cost this campaign one verification pass.**
The shipped fused kernel computes `softmax((q·kᵀ + mask)·scale)·v` — **mask BEFORE scale** — and
production compensates at `tenstorrent.py:7205` (see `taped_ttnn.py:866-880`). **ttml's kernel is
mask AFTER scale.** Getting this backwards produces a wrong gradient that the FORWARD AGREES WITH,
and it is measured: error flat at 3.3e-02 under the right reading, growing 3.3e-02 → 8.3e-01 with
mask magnitude under the wrong one. **Write the mask-magnitude sweep as your first control, before
any speed number.** A reference written the conventional way agrees with the wrong gradient.

**Release-gated end to end.** The forward change touches the kernel serving **560 of 560**
triangle-attention calls on the Boltz-2 512 aa fold
(`perf/sizegate/baseline/census_boltz2_512_p300c.json`). Moritz's inference constraint is a hard
