OF3T row `of3t-maskaudit` — **did the campaign's activation-space readings mask their padding, or
not?** One question, enumerated over every reading, answered from artifacts rather than prose.

## Why this row exists

D95, pass 176. Scoring the same two tensors over a 384-padded activation versus over the 56 real
tokens gave:

    pair track z                  over 384x384 (padded)   over 56x56 (real)   dilution
    a STRUCTURAL difference             0.005374               0.21040          39.2x
    a NUMERICAL difference (bf16)       0.019607               0.02441           1.2x

**Padding is not a uniform scale-down.** Numerical noise is generated everywhere, including the
padded rows; a structural difference is generated only where the model does real work. So padding
deflates the structural term by roughly the padding ratio and the numerical term hardly at all.
On the padded tensor the comparison reads *"the two agree to within bf16"* (0.27x). On the real
tokens it reads *"8.6x worse than bf16"*. **Same tensors, opposite conclusion, and the wrong one
is the comforting one.**

That matters most for **A18** — the rule that a disagreeing forward invalidates the gradient.
A18 exists to catch a **wrong transform**, which is exactly the class padding hides, and it is
fully sensitive to the imprecision it does not need to catch.

## What is already known, so you do not redo it

`of3t-trunkfwd` **did mask**: every tensor in its artifacts is named `*_masked` and
`UPSTREAM_FLOOR.json` records `tokens 64, real_tokens 56`. So the trunk readings, including the
46.67x, are **not** exposed. That is one reading of many and it is the only one I checked.

## What to do

**1. ENUMERATE.** Every activation-space reading the campaign relies on — the A18 forwards above
all, plus any forward comparison quoted in `THE_ANSWER.json`, `SCOREBOARD_PASS_175.json` or a
row's own headline. For each, open **the artifact that produced it**, not the write-up, and
record MASKED or PADDED. A write-up saying "masked" is not evidence; a tensor named `*_masked`
with a `real_tokens` field beside it is.

**2. PADDING.** For each, the padding fraction as real/total tokens. A reading with no padding
is not exposed and should be marked so explicitly rather than omitted.

**3. RECOMPUTE.** Every PADDED reading, recomputed restricted to real tokens in **both**
numerator and denominator. Publish old and new side by side. **Do not scale** — the dilution is
not uniform across error types and a scale factor would be a guess.

**4. A18.** The pass/fail table before and after. If no verdict changes, that is the result and
it is a good one — say it in your first sentence. If any changes, say which and by how much.

**5. CONTROL.** Two, both measured: a reading known to be masked must recompute **unchanged**
(if it moves, your recomputation is wrong, not the reading), and a deliberately unmasked variant
of that same reading must **move** (if it does not, your mask is not being applied).
