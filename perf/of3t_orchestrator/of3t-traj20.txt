## What §7 fixes in advance, and none of it is yours to choose

- **N = 20**, fixed at pass 1. It exceeds the gradient-accumulation cycle so clipping and
  optimizer state engage, and every step sits inside the 1000-step linear warmup so the LR moves
  on all 20 and a frozen schedule shows immediately.
- **Compare the UPDATE, not the weights** (§7a). `d_k = w_k - w_0` per tensor, scored as
  `||d_k_ours - d_k_ref||_2 / (||d_k_ref||_2 + 1e-30)`. This is load-bearing: with warmup to
  `max_lr=1e-3` from `base_lr=0`, 20 steps move the weights by a relative ~1e-4, so a relative L2
  on `w_k` is dominated by `w_0` — identical on both stacks **by construction** — and would read
  ~0 for a correct implementation *and* for one computing garbage. Comparing weights makes this
  entire instrument vacuous.
- **k = 1 is not a rung, and not free either** (A10). `lr(1)` is exactly 0, so a correct
  implementation has `d_1 = 0` and any two correct implementations agree bit-exactly — including
  with one computing garbage, which is why it cannot be quoted as a pass. But `of3t-gradients`
  measured **our `d_1` at 0.0139 where theirs is exactly 0**, because `recipes.py:119` wires
  `af3_lr(s)` while `AdamW.step` increments before it reads: our step 1 ran at `lr(1)` where
  theirs runs at `lr(0)`. **Report `d_1` for both sides and treat any non-zero value as a defect
  to localise.** D11 is recorded CLOSED by `of3t-updaterule` — this run is the end-to-end check
  that it really is.
- **Discrimination starts at k = 2** (A9). `d_2 = 7.309e-04` in absolute terms is a real update
  whose relative comparison is well-posed; the earlier reasoning that it "reads the tolerance"
  conflated the size of the update with the precision of comparing it.
- **The bar is the SHAPE, not a magnitude.** Linear or sub-linear growth in k passes;
  **super-linear FAILS at any magnitude, including inside the per-step bars.** Do not convert
  this into a threshold on `d_20`.

## Controls

Two, both measured. A control that breaks the comparison; and an arm showing the instrument
**can fail** — deliberately mis-wire one component (apply the LR after the step, or feed the
optimizer stale gradients) and demonstrate super-linear divergence. §3e's rule applies here: a
bar that has not been shown capable of failing is not evidence.
