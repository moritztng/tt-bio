**A forward that agrees beside a gradient 5.4x too large is a statement about the BACKWARD.**
That is this row's whole premise and it is what makes the question tractable.

Two further facts you do not need to re-derive:
- the forward and the gradient order the two bias conventions **oppositely** — pre-scaling
  improves the forward 6.30x and degrades the gradient 1.65x — so **do not flip D1** looking for
  relief; it makes the gradient worse, and it is held for Angstrom reasons anyway.
- the shipped convention's smaller gradient comes from delivering the bias at 0.204 of reference
  size, i.e. **by doing less of the model's work**. Do not treat any arm that shrinks the
  gradient by shrinking the computation as an improvement; the campaign's standing no-cheat rule
  applies and `of3t-trunkgrad` already refused this once.

## What to do

1. **LOCUS** — which backward op creates it. Compare **medians per leaf op**, never the worst
   tensor: the worst tensor names the tail, not the locus. `pair_transition` is known to move
   3.756e-01 -> 5.317e-01 between the conventions, so it is a starting point, not an answer.
2. **FORWARD_AGREES** — re-confirm the forward at that leaf **in the same process** that takes
   the gradient. The contrast is the evidence; if the two come from different boundaries there
   is no contrast.
3. **NORM** — the error is 5.41x too LARGE in norm with cosine 0.137. Decompose the squared
   error into magnitude and direction and say which dominates, the way `of3t-trunk043ref` did
   for the forward (it found 74.88 % magnitude / 25.12 % direction there). A cosine of 0.137 is
   nearly orthogonal and that is a strong clue: it is not a scale error.
4. **CONTROL** — the four above are already measured; re-run them in your process rather than
   carrying them in. `of3t-auxgrad` showed that an in-process reproduction of a predecessor's
   arm is the strongest instrument evidence available, and it cost one extra run.
5. **FIX_OR_NOT** — standing candidates from the campaign's own record, to be **tested not
   assumed**: `tape-recomputes-what-a-fused-kernel-computed` (a chunked recompute in the backward
   that the fused forward did differently), `hallgrad-tape-self-closure-leak`, and the
   possibility that a backward is simply missing for one op and silently returning something
   plausible. "There is no single locus, the error is diffuse" is a legitimate pre-registered
   outcome — say it plainly if that is what the medians show.
