## What to do

1. **LOCUS** — which backward op creates it. Compare **medians per leaf op**, never the worst
   tensor: the worst tensor names the tail, not the locus. `pair_transition` is known to move
   3.756e-01 -> 5.317e-01 between the conventions, so it is a starting point, not an answer.
2. **FORWARD_AGREES** — re-confirm the forward at that leaf **in the same process** that takes
   the gradient. The contrast is the evidence; if the two come from different boundaries there
   is no contrast.
3. **NORM** — the error is 5.41x too LARGE in norm with cosine 0.137. Decompose the squared
   error into magnitude and direction and say which dominates, the way `of3t-trunk043ref` did
   for the forward (it found 74.88 % magnitude / 25.12 % direction there). A cosine of 0.137 is
   nearly orthogonal and that is a strong clue: it is not a scale error.
4. **CONTROL** — the four above are already measured; re-run them in your process rather than
   carrying them in. `of3t-auxgrad` showed that an in-process reproduction of a predecessor's
   arm is the strongest instrument evidence available, and it cost one extra run.
5. **FIX_OR_NOT** — standing candidates from the campaign's own record, to be **tested not
   assumed**: `tape-recomputes-what-a-fused-kernel-computed` (a chunked recompute in the backward
   that the fused forward did differently), `hallgrad-tape-self-closure-leak`, and the
   possibility that a backward is simply missing for one op and silently returning something
   plausible. "There is no single locus, the error is diffuse" is a legitimate pre-registered
   outcome — say it plainly if that is what the medians show.

leading hypothesis — but it is a **hypothesis to test, not a premise to build on.**

## AMENDMENT 2 (of3t-orchestrator, pass 181) — append-only. An exclusion you can use, from your own numbers.

Arithmetic over the figures **you** published, no new measurement, offered as a narrowing rather
than a conclusion — check it against what you measure next rather than adopting it.

**Your chain is worse than PERFECTLY COHERENT accumulation of your own per-block error.**

    per-block backward alone                      4.125325e-02
    48 INDEPENDENT errors of that size (sqrt N)   0.285811
    48 PERFECTLY COHERENT ones (linear N)         1.980156
    your MEASURED chain of 48                     5.351880   -> 18.73x sqrt(N), 2.70x linear

Adding up 48 copies of the per-block error, all pointing the same way, still falls **2.70x
short** of what you measured. That needs no conversion and no assumption about direction — it is
a ratio of two numbers you already published.

**So two mechanisms are now excluded, not one.** Your per-block test already ruled out *a leaf
computes a wrong gradient from correct inputs*. This rules out *each block adds its own small
error*. What survives is an operator that **multiplies the incoming cotangent** by something the
reference's does not: a block handed a correct cotangent behaves (4.1e-02), handed a wrong one it
makes it worse. **Look for a Jacobian applied to the ARRIVING gradient, not for a leaf's own
output.**

Weaker, and flagged as such because it assumes the error is roughly orthogonal to the signal:
converting your cosines via `cos = 1/sqrt(1+r^2)` gives error/signal **0.0049 -> 7.2140 ->
65.6135** at 1/24/48 blocks, i.e. multiplicative growth of **1.3732** per block early and
**1.0964** late. That is **not** a clean exponential and no single growth constant should be
quoted — what survives is only the sign, `g > 1` in both halves.

Nothing else in the brief changes, including: do not flip D1, and do not bank an arm that shrinks
the gradient by shrinking the computation.
