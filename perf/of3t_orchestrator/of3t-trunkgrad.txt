## What to do

**Take the gradient against a 0.4.3-built float64 reference, under BOTH conventions.** The
1.061553e+01 on record came from `of3t-pairformer` scored against a **0.5.0** reference and is
superseded; do not carry it in, and do not carry in any other figure — `of3t-auxgrad` found that
re-running the old arm in-process reproduced its predecessor **to every digit**, and that
cross-process agreement is what made its new number trustworthy. Do the same here: an arm that
reproduces `of3t-pairformer`'s configuration is your instrument control.

Both conventions, because the campaign needs to know what the held default costs **in gradient
terms**, not only in Angstrom and forward-relative-L2. That number does not exist today and it is
the missing half of the D1 decision.

**Report by MASS, not by count** (A23): how much of the compared mass sits outside the per-tensor
bar, beside how many tensors do. `of3t-auxgrad`'s scope had 89 of 176 tensors over the bar
holding 1.17e-07 of the mass — the counts and the mass said opposite things and only the mass
decided the scope.

**A26's `sqrt(2)` does NOT apply here.** Against a float64 reference only one side carries error,
so the reachable bar is the threshold itself — see the **A26-SCOPE** amendment, which
`of3t-auxgrad` surfaced. Quote it if you like, but do not let it rescue a tensor.

**Confirm the forward in the same process that takes the gradient**, both tracks, masked, with
the padding fraction beside each figure.
