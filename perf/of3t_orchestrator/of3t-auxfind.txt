## What to do

**Start from the head that PASSES.** `distogram_logits` clears the bar by 14x while four siblings
fail by 6-10x. They share a trunk: the same `pairformer_stack` inside the confidence head, the
same `embed_zij`, the same inputs. **What do the four failing heads do that distogram does not?**
That contrast is the whole lead and it is cheaper than instrumenting all five.

**Locate by MEDIAN per leaf op, not by the worst tensor** — the worst tensor names the tail, not
the locus. Walk the confidence head's leaves and report the median disagreement grouped by leaf
name, so a single bad op separates from a diffuse one.

**Two specific suspects, both from reading, neither confirmed** — test them, do not assume:
- `prediction_heads.embed_zij` runs at ambient dtype in 0.4.3 (0.5.0 added an fp32 autocast and
  0.4.3 wrapped it the other way). If our port runs it in bf16 where the reference runs fp32,
  that is a precision defect on a path feeding all five heads — but distogram passes, so it
  cannot be the whole story.
- the confidence `pairformer_stack` is a **second** Pairformer instance with its own flags.
  `precision-lever-on-for-one-submodel` is a standing defect class here: `s_fp32_residual` is
  True for the confidence Pairformer and False for the trunk. Check the shipped flags of the
  confidence stack against the reference's behaviour, at the call site, not the default.
