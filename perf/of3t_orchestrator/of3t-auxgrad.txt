## What is settled

`of3t-auxfind` returned **GO**: `aux_heads`' A18 forward failure is caused by our confidence
Pairformer running **unmasked**, and passing the reference's masks — `pair_mask` into both
triangle multiplications and onto the pair transition's output, the additive `-1e9` companion
into both triangle attentions, and `single_mask` into the host s-path's attention scores and its
transition output — takes A18 from **4 of 5 heads over the bar, worst 5.174368e-01**, to **0 of
5, worst 3.865648e-03**. That is 12.9x inside the 5.0e-02 bar and 18.3x inside A26's reachable
bar, with every norm ratio within 0.25 % of 1 and every cosine at 0.99999 or better. The
"maybe the heads are simply imprecise" alternative was **refuted by measurement**, not skipped.

## Deliverable 1 — the gradient, because A18 does not bound it

**A18 says a disagreeing forward INVALIDATES a gradient. An agreeing forward merely stops
invalidating it** (A18 addendum, D9). The **2.2996e-03** headline over the **2.843136 %** of
squared gradient norm this scope holds was carried in from `of3t-auxheads` and measured on the
taped `forward_device` path **before** the fix existed. So the repaired forward and the gradient
on record are **of different functions**, and the scope is currently neither void nor passing —
it is **UNMEASURED**, which is how the scoreboard now carries it.

Re-take it with the fix **on**, against the same 0.4.3 reference and the same boundary, and score
it against **5.0e-02 per-tensor / 2.0e-02 mass-weighted**, and against A26's `sqrt(2) x`
reachable bar. Report the mass-weighted headline and the worst tensor **located**, not just its
value. **Confirm the forward reads 3.865648e-03 in the same process** — carrying numbers in
across processes is exactly what made this re-take necessary, so do not do it again.

## Deliverable 2 — does this reach users, MEASURED this time

`of3t-auxfind` states that the shipped `_confidence` call site at `openfold3_fold.py:260` passes
no `token_mask`, and that whether a user-facing fold's token axis carries a padded position is a
property of the feature pipeline it **read from source rather than measured on a real target**.
If it does carry padding, this change moves **pLDDT, PAE, PTM/IPTM and the ranking score** on
ordinary folds.

**Measure it.** Run a real target end to end both ways and report the deltas. If the token axis
is never padded on a shipped path, **say so plainly — that is the reassuring branch and it is
just as valuable**; report it as prominently as the alarming one. This campaign escalated a
product claim from a source reading once and had to withdraw it (**D90**); do not repeat that.
A confidence delta is scored against **seed scatter**, not against zero
(`conf-scatter-bar`): quote the same-arm different-seed spread beside any number.
