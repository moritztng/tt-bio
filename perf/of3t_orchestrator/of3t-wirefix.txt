## What `of3t-traj20` found (VERDICT NO-GO), and it pre-registered all four before measuring

Every one is a component applied in the wrong order, called at the wrong time, or not called at
all — the class no per-parameter gradient agreement can reach.

1. **Weight decay upstream does not have.** `recipes.py:118` builds `AdamW(...)` and takes
   `weight_decay=0.01` by default; upstream's `configure_optimizers` builds `torch.optim.Adam`
   with none (`runner.py:852-860`). Removing it alone: endpoint **2.938e-01 → 1.105e-01**,
   exponent **+0.406 → −0.152**. Categorical tell: **74 tensors have an exactly zero reference
   update** and `lr*wd*theta` moves every one of them — with wd on, **0 of 4,147** are
   bit-identical at k=2; with it off, **76** are.
2. **Per-sample clipping implemented, tested, never called.** `per_sample_clipping: True` at
   `clip_val 10.0` is upstream's shipped OF3 default (`model_config.py:156-159`).
   `AdamW.clip_and_accumulate` implements it and has **0 callers in `tt_bio/`** against 3 in
   `tests/test_clipping_matches_openfold3.py`; `train_loop` clips the batch once instead. At
   k=20 our batch norm 1.5899e+01 takes coefficient **0.6290** where each of upstream's four
   samples takes **1.0** — and per-sample clipping changes the **direction** of the sum.
3. **A participation count written and never read.** `AdamW` writes `self.participation`
   (`optim.py:411`) and reads it **0 times**; upstream's `_sync_and_average_grads` divides each
   parameter's accumulated gradient by its own count (`grad_manager.py:225-232`). Counts spread
   **[1,4]** at k=20 because the confidence head is disabled where the confidence weight is
   zero. Closing it on top of 1 and 2: k=2 **1.084e-01 → 8.715e-03** (12.4x), worst tensor
   1.507e+06 → 1.130e-02. Adam does **not** cancel it — §7a-bis measured 2.547e-07 for a 1.01x
   scaling, but at 4x `eps = 1e-8` stops cancelling.
4. **The wrong LR schedule family.** `recipes.py:119` calls `af3_lr(s, lr, warmup_steps=...)`
   with no `plateau_until`, selecting Protenix's schedule where OpenFold3 ships AF2's. Over
   0..200,004 in float64: **200,004 of 200,005 exact**, one mismatch at step 50,000 (ours
   0.00171, theirs 0.0018, 5.0e-02 relative). `plateau_until=50000` makes it 200,005 of 200,005.
   **The 20-step window cannot see this** — both families share the 0..1000 linear warmup — so
   fix it, and verify it with §4's whole-domain method, not with the trajectory.
