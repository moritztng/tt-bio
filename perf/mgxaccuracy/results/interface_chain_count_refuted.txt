THE 100-RESIDUE DIAL CANNOT BE DERIVED FROM CONTACT, AND THE DERIVED DIAL RUNS BACKWARDS
==========================================================================================

2026-09-24 23:5xZ. No new folds -- banked design outputs and the fixtures they were designed
against, read on whglx with `perf/mgxaccuracy/contact.py --per-chain`, written this pass.

WHY THIS WAS ATTEMPTED

`results/chain_census_all_cells.txt` carries the row's chain-count headline and names its own
load-bearing caveat: the headline holds only because GroEL 512/off560's 24-residue C fragment
is called not-a-chain, by a 100-residue threshold "not derived from anything". Counted as two
chains it is a multi-chain cell at 87.5 % and the sentence is false.

A chain the binder never comes near is arguably not part of the design problem whatever its
length, so the threshold looked replaceable by a geometric dial: how many target chains the
delivered binder actually TOUCHES, a property of the structure rather than of a hand-picked
number. That was the hypothesis. It is refuted.

THE MEASUREMENT

Contact = any atom pair within 5.0 A. n_touched = target chains with at least one contact.
Binder = the chain with 80 residues, the rule `scrmsd.py:designed_chain` uses (see DEFECTS).
Chain boundaries are the FIXTURE's, imposed in file order via `--target-chains` (see DEFECTS).

REFUTED, AND IT RUNS THE WRONG WAY AT BOTH ENDS

  * The cell whose binders are MOST confined to a single chain is GroEL 1536/off0 -- 8 of 8
    binders touch exactly one of its three chains -- and it returns 0 of 8 under the bar.
  * The cell whose binders MOST often span two chains is GroEL 512/off560, 5 of 8, and it is
    the best cell in the row at 87.5 %.

Neither cell carries a merge artefact: both fixtures are unmerged and their output chain
labels match the fixture exactly, so the two observations the refutation rests on need no
correction. Interface chain count does not order the outcomes and does not replace the
threshold. The census keeps its caveat, and the caveat is now known to be irreducible by this
route rather than merely unexamined.

WHAT SURVIVES, STATED NARROWLY

The census's correlation is over FIXTURE chain count and is untouched by this: every
multi-chain fixture crop except the disputed 512/off560 still returns 0 %. What is gone is the
obvious MECHANISM for it -- "the binder must be designed against a seam between two chains" --
because at GroEL 1536/off0 every binder is designed against one chain's surface and nothing
refolds anyway. That is independent of, and agrees with, FAS 1024/off0: a single-chain target,
0 of 8. Do not upgrade this into "extent is the carrier"; FAS 1536 single chain reads 50 %,
so extent does not order the cells either.

Docking is not the failure mode and the 0 % cells are not undocked. All eight FAS 1024/off0
binders are IN CONTACT at min d 0.75-1.73 A with 1316-1885 contacts each -- MORE contact than
FAS 1536/off0, the 50 % cell, at 337-1383. Consistent with the note already in
`results/contact_512_gpb.txt`: docking and designability are different axes.

TWO DEFECTS IN THE RECORD, BOTH FOUND BY THIS PASS
----------------------------------------------------

1. `contact.py` IDENTIFIED THE WRONG CHAIN AS THE BINDER whenever a target fragment is shorter
   than the binder. It took the chain with the FEWEST residues, so on GroEL 512/off560
   (binder 80, B 488, C 24) it took the 24-residue target fragment and measured the target
   against itself, reporting 3 of 8 designs as touching nothing. Fixed: `--binder-res 80`
   selects by exact residue count, the same rule `scrmsd.py:designed_chain` uses, and refuses
   rather than guesses when the count is not unique. Every quality number in this row comes
   through `scrmsd.py` and is unaffected; the banked `results/contact_512_gpb.txt` is also
   unaffected, being a single-chain target where smallest and binder coincide.

2. THE CENSUS MISSED A CHAIN. GroEL 1024/off560 is 488 + 524 + 12, THREE target chains, not
   the "B 488 + C 524" the census records -- its own numbers sum to 1012, not 1024. The
   12-residue D fragment fell under the same 100-residue threshold. The cell stays multi-chain
   and stays at 0 %, so no headline moves, but it is a second fragment the dial silently ate.

3. Not a defect, but a trap worth the line: BOLTZGEN'S DESIGN WRITER MERGES ADJACENT TARGET
   CHAINS. The GroEL 1536/off0 fixture is 524 + 524 + 488 and the design is written as
   1048 + 488, so chain counts read off a design output undercount. The fixture is
   authoritative. `--target-chains` imposes the fixture's sizes in file order and checks the
   total; as a control it reproduces GroEL 1024/off0 (unmerged) exactly, 1:6/8 2:2/8, and it
   left GroEL 1536/off0 at 8/8 on one chain, so the merge never affected that reading either.
