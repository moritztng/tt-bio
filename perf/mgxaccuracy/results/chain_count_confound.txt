GroEL's chains are ~524 residues, so ANY crop of this target above ~524 residues necessarily
adds a second chain. The size ladder and the chain count are therefore not separable on this
target, and the separation is perfect: the only two cells without a genuine second chain are
the only two cells that refold.

THE ROW HAS ALREADY SEPARATED THEM ONCE, ON THE OTHER TARGET, AND THE CARRIER WAS THE CHAIN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

`results/gpb_supporting.txt`, banked before this pass: 1GPB's chains are ~1008 residues, long
enough to hold a big single-chain crop, and the 512 -> 1536 size effect decomposes into

    +3.33 A   for 823 residues of ONE chain          does not reject
   +14.77 A   for adding chain B at the same crop    rejects

So on the target where the two CAN be separated, adding a chain carried the effect and stretching
one chain to 823 residues did not. That is one target and one decomposition, and "does not
reject" at n=8 is not "is zero" — but it points the opposite way from a residue-count rule.

WHAT THIS CHANGES AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
---------------------------------------

DOES NOT CHANGE: every number in QUALITY. The cells are what they are and the ladder still says
a user who crops GroEL to 512 gets designs that refold and one who crops to 1024 or 1536 does
not. The practical instruction is unaffected for anyone cropping GroEL.

DOES CHANGE what the instruction is ABOUT. "Crop to about 512 residues" is a residue-count rule
read off a ladder that cannot distinguish residue count from chain count, and the one experiment
that did distinguish them chose the chain. A user with a 700-residue SINGLE-CHAIN target is told
by the residue rule to crop away 200 residues, on no evidence that applies to their case, while
a user with two 300-residue chains is told they are fine when the chain rule would warn them.

WHAT THE 768 RUNG DOES AND DOES NOT SETTLE. Both 768 crops carry a second partial chain (244 and
280 residues), so 768 adds residues AND chain content over 512 and cannot separate them either.
It remains a valid CEILING measurement, which is what `plans/groel_768.txt` claims for it, and
it is not a test of this confound. Do not read it as one.

THE CONTROL THAT WOULD SETTLE IT cannot be built on GroEL: a single-chain crop above ~524
residues does not exist there. It has to be a long-chain target, which is 1GPB, where the
823-residue single-chain cell already exists. A second single-chain rung at ~1000 residues on
1GPB, against the 823 cell and against a two-chain crop of the same total size, is the
experiment. Not launched this pass: the two cards this row holds are running the 768 rung and
the brief caps it at three chips.
