WHY THIS IS THE REFUTED BRANCH AND NOT THE INCONCLUSIVE ONE. closing_rule.txt gives two
independent ways to refute. The magnitude clause does not fire: |interaction| = 2.11 A is under
the conservative 7.2 A band, which on its own would only be INCONCLUSIVE. The second clause
does fire, and it is the clause that was written for exactly this: "both size simple effects
share a sign with both outside the repeatability band". +14.75 and +12.65 share a sign and are
both ~7x the 1.8 A worst-case repeat. So the structure under test — each factor's two simple
effects pointing OPPOSITE ways — is absent, and absent in the direction that says something
rather than in the direction of a null.

WHAT REPLACES IT. On 1GPB size was +14.65 at one crop and -4.51 at the other, and crop was
+13.04 at one size and -6.13 at the other: four significant effects, all cancelling. On GroEL
size is the whole story (+13.70 main, both simple effects reject) and crop is nothing (-0.17
main, neither rejects). Two proteins, two different structures, and the only thing they agree
on is that 1536 does not refold: 1GPB 19.398/13.273, GroEL 15.606/14.387, 0 of 8 under 4 A in
all four of those cells.
