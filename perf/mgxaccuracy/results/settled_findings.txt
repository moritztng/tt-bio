WHAT "SIZE" MEANS IN THIS ROW, moved out of the state doc header
=================================================================

TARGET residues at a FIXED 80-residue binder. Every boltzgen cell in this row holds the binder
at 80, so the binder's share of the token axis falls from 13.5% at 512 tokens to 5.0% at 1536
as the target grows. Those two move together in every cell and the row has never separated
them.

It scopes the claim rather than weakening any comparison:

  - The METRIC is unaffected. `designfolding-bb_rmsd` is an 80-residue backbone RMSD at every
    rung, so it is not measuring a bigger object at a bigger size.
  - The FAS CONTROL is unaffected. It holds the binder at 80 exactly as GroEL and 1GPB did, so
    the chain-vs-residues comparison is not confounded by it.

The only place a different binder appears anywhere in the row is pxdesign's 1224-token
production case, which uses 200 residues and is already flagged in
`results/pxdesign_size_ladder.txt` as a different configuration and NOT a rung.

`docs/design-throughput.md` states the 80-residue binder to the user, so nobody reads the crop
advice as covering a longer one.
