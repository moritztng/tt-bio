WHAT THE 1024 CELL DOES SAY, WITHIN FAS. A single chain of 1024 residues returns NOTHING under

THIS WEAKENS THE CHAIN HYPOTHESIS AND THE ROW SHOULD SAY SO PLAINLY. The reason FAS was built
(`results/chain_count_confound.txt`) is that on GroEL and 1GPB every cell above 512 residues
also carries a second chain, so the row could not tell a residue-count ceiling from a
chain-count one. If chain count were the whole story, a single chain should have kept designing
where two chains stopped. At 1024 it does not: FAS single-chain 1024 is 0 of 8, exactly like
GroEL two-chain 1024. The 768 rung pointed the other way (2 of 8 against GroEL's 0 of 8) and is
still INTERMEDIATE, so the two rungs disagree in direction and neither is decisive.

The pre-registered decision is the 1536 cell and it stays pre-registered: CHAIN COUNT IS THE
CARRIER needs 512 -> 1536 to NOT reject AND >= 4 of 8 under the bar at 1536. With 1024 already
at 0 of 8 and 512 -> 1024 rejecting, that clause now looks unlikely to fire — stated here,
before the cell lands, so it cannot be presented afterwards as something the row expected all
along.
