WHAT THIS SETTLES
-----------------

768 IS ALREADY COLLAPSED, on both crops, and the pre-registered clause fires cleanly this time:
median 15.226 and 12.984 A against the 4.0 A bar, 0 of 8 under it in both cells, and 512->768
rejects at both crops. At offset 0 the separation is PERFECT, U = 0.0 of 64, p = 0.0002 — not a
single one of the eight 768 designs is as good as the worst of the eight at 512.

The clause bodies in plans/groel_768.txt are disjoint by construction, so unlike the 1024 rung
there is one verdict and not two. COLLAPSED BY 768, both crops, RUNG VERDICT COLLAPSED BY 768.

**The usable ceiling is therefore [512, 768), not [512, 1024).** Two passes ago it was an
interpolation across 1024 residues; it is now bracketed within 256.

THE LADDER ABOVE THE CEILING IS NOT MONOTONE, AND IT IS REPORTED
------------------------------------------------------------------

At offset 0, 768 -> 1024 is -6.509 A and REJECTS (U = 61.0 of 64, p = 0.0011). 1024 reads
significantly BETTER than 768 there, while 1536 reads worse again:

    offset 0    512  0.852  ->  768  15.226  ->  1024  8.718  ->  1536  15.606
    offset 560  512  1.739  ->  768  12.984  ->  1024 14.457  ->  1536  14.387

Offset 560 is monotone-ish and rejects nothing above 768. Offset 0 is not, and the dip at 1024
is large enough that the rank test separates it. Two things to say about that, and neither
rescues a size rule above the ceiling:

  - Every cell at 768, 1024 and 1536 returns 0 of 8 under 4 A, on both crops. Above the ceiling
    the metric is measuring HOW BADLY a design fails to refold, not whether a user gets
    something. 8.7 A and 15.2 A are both "returns nothing usable", and ordering two failures is
    not the same as ordering two outcomes.
  - It is not chain count: 768/off0 is A 524 + B 244 and 1024/off0 is A 524 + B 500, both two
    chains (`results/chain_count_confound.txt`). The 768 cell's second chain is a 244-residue
    fragment and the 1024 cell's is nearly complete, which is a candidate and is not tested.

So the row does NOT claim a monotone size curve. It claims a ceiling, measured at both crops
with the rank test, and says that above it the designs do not refold whatever the exact number
reads.

carry a second chain, exactly as plans/groel_768.txt said in advance. That control is
plans/chain_vs_residues.txt on targets/fas_chainA_1962.cif, built this pass and unrun. And it
says nothing about upstream, which is the qb2 pair's question.
