WHAT THE ROW TAKES FROM THIS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
--------------------------------------------------

TAKES: 1024 IS NOT USABLE, on both crops, and this is not a reading that depends on which
clause you apply. Medians 8.718 and 14.457 A against a 4.0 A bar, 0 of 8 under it in both
cells, and 512->1024 rejects at both crops (p=0.0070 at offset 0, p=0.0030 at 560). The
ceiling the doc ships is therefore in [512, 1024) and "crop to about 512" stands, now with a
measured rung under it instead of an interpolation across 1024 residues.

DOES NOT TAKE: the SHAPE of the fall. The two crops disagree about it and the plan's clauses
overlap on exactly that point, so neither the data nor the pre-registration settles it.

  - At offset 0 both steps reject: +7.866 A for 512->1024 and +6.888 A for 1024->1536, about
    half the total +14.754 A each. That is a continuous decay with no cliff.
  - At offset 560 only the first step rejects: +12.718 A for 512->1024, then -0.070 A for
    1024->1536 (U=39/64, p=0.5054). 1024 is already at the 1536 level. That is a cliff at or
    below 1024.

THE PRE-REGISTRATION HAS A DEFECT AND IT IS REPORTED, NOT RESOLVED AFTER THE FACT.
plans/groel_1024.txt states COLLAPSED BY 1024 as "median > 4.0 A AND <= 1 of 8 under 4.0 A AND
512-vs-1024 rejects at 0.05", which both cells satisfy, and ladder_read.py — committed 83
minutes before the cells landed — applies exactly that and prints COLLAPSED BY 1024 for both
crops and for the rung. But the same plan's INTERMEDIATE clause lists "both contrasts rejecting
(a continuous decay with no cliff)" among the things it covers, and at offset 0 both contrasts
do reject. The two clauses overlap and the plan does not say which wins.

So the rung's verdict is stated twice, deliberately:

  by the clause bodies, which is what the committed reader applies   COLLAPSED BY 1024
  by the INTERMEDIATE prose at offset 0, which the reader does not   INTERMEDIATE

Both give the doc the same sentence, because both say 1024 is not usable and neither moves the
ceiling out of [512, 1024). Choosing between them after seeing the numbers would be worth
nothing, so it is not done. plans/groel_768.txt, written before its own cells exist, does not
repeat the overlap: its clause bodies are disjoint and nothing outside them decides.

WHERE THIS GOES NEXT. The bracket [512, 1024) is 512 residues wide and the doc's number sits at
its bottom end, which costs a user with a 700-residue target a crop they may not need. 768 at
both crops halves it. Launched this pass on the same tree.

WHAT THIS RUNG DOES NOT SPEAK TO. Upstream. Every cell here is a device cell and the question
of whether the device is WORSE than upstream at a given size is the qb2 fp32 pair's, still
computing.
