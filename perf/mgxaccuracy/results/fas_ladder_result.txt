WHAT THE FOUR LANDED CELLS ALREADY SAY, within this target
-----------------------------------------------------------

A single chain of 1024 residues returns NOTHING under the 4 A bar, the same count every
multi-chain cell at 1024 and above has returned on both other targets, and the fall from its
own 512 cell rejects. The decay is gradual rather than a cliff — 4 of 8, then 2 of 8, then
0 of 8 — with neither adjacent step separating and only the two-step contrast doing so.

That WEAKENS the chain hypothesis this control was built to test. If chain count were the whole
story, one chain should have kept designing where two stopped; at 1024 it does not. The 768
rung points the other way (2 of 8 against GroEL's 0 of 8 at the same residue count) and is
INTERMEDIATE, so the two rungs disagree in direction and neither is decisive. Details and the
cross-target caveat in `results/fas_768_result.txt` and `results/fas_1024_result.txt`.

The bottom rung REPLICATES across windows: offset 900 shares ZERO residues with offset 0 at 512
and reads 3.029 A / 5 of 8 against 3.782 A / 4 of 8. The cell that decides whether the 1024
finding replicates is 1024/off900, and `plans/fas_offset900.txt` pre-registers three clauses
for it, one of which obliges the row to WITHDRAW `results/fas_1024_result.txt`'s sentence in
the words it was published in. Read it with `fas_read.py --replication`.

READ WITHIN TARGET ONLY. FAS is a third protein. Its 512 cell at 3.782 A sits between GroEL's
0.852 and 1GPB's worst 512 window at 17.785, and that spread is the between-target variation
this row published long before FAS existed — not evidence about size or chains.
