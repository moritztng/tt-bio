A PREDICTION I PUBLISHED TWICE AND LOST
----------------------------------------

`results/fas_1024_result.txt` and the state doc both said CHAIN COUNT IS THE CARRIER "now looks
unlikely to fire", on the strength of the 1024 cell being 0 of 8 — and that reading was
reinforced when the 1024 finding REPLICATED on a second window at 21.003 A, also 0 of 8. It
fired anyway. The clause was pre-registered in `plans/chain_vs_residues.txt` before any FAS cell
existed, its two conditions are 512->1536 not rejecting AND >= 4 of 8 under the bar at 1536,
and both hold: p = 0.6454 and 4 of 8. The verdict is the plan's, not mine, which is the entire
reason the plan was written first.

-------------------------------------------------------------------

The 1536 crop CONTAINS the 512 and 1024 crops as prefixes: the same residues that returned
nothing usable at 1024 return four designs under 4 A when more of the same chain is added. So
the 1024 dip is the anomaly here, not the 1536 recovery, and a single median per size is not
describing a monotone decay on this target. GroEL's offset-0 ladder was non-monotone above its
ceiling too (0.852 -> 15.226 -> 8.718 -> 15.606), which the row already reported; the
difference is that there every cell above 512 was 0 of 8, so the wobble was among failures.
Here it moves across the usability bar.

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT LICENCE

DOES: on this target, a single chain still designs at 1536 residues — 3.274 A with 4 of 8 under
the bar and 2 of 8 under the STRICT 2 A bar — where every multi-chain 1536 cell this row has
measured, four across two targets, returned 0 of 8. That is the clause firing and it is the
strongest evidence the row has that the published size axis is carried by chain count.

DOES NOT: it does not make the ladder monotone, it does not explain the 1024 dip, and it does
not transfer to GroEL or 1GPB, whose chains cannot hold a single-chain crop above ~524 and 823
residues. `docs/design-throughput.md` must be re-read against this before it ships another
sentence about chains — it currently says a single chain "does not buy back the size", written
when 1024 was the last cell in.

NEXT: the 1024 dip is worth one more cell. A single-chain 1280 rung, or a third window at 1024,
would say whether the dip is a window artefact or a real trough. Pre-register before running.
