**Every multi-chain crop in this row returned zero designs under the bar.** Counted exactly:
EIGHT distinct multi-chain crops on TWO valid targets (GroEL 768/1024/1536 at two offsets each,
gpb 1536 at two offsets), measured as eleven cells because the gpb crops were re-run on several
engine trees. `big_1831` is excluded throughout — it is the disqualified disconnected fixture.
Single-chain crops run from 0 % to 87.5 %, with eight of twelve cells above zero.

The distinction matters and this file got it wrong once: the first draft said "twelve of them"
on "three targets", which counted re-runs of the same crop as separate evidence and counted the
disqualified fixture as a target. Crops are the unit; a crop re-run on a second engine tree is
a control, not another observation.

THREE CAVEATS, AND THE FIRST IS LOAD-BEARING
----------------------------------------------

1. THE CLAIM DEPENDS ON CALLING A 24-RESIDUE FRAGMENT NOT-A-CHAIN. GroEL 512/off560 is
   B 488 + C 24. Counted as one chain plus a stub it is the BEST cell in the row at 87.5 %;
   counted as two chains it is a multi-chain cell at 87.5 % and the headline above is false.
   The 100-residue threshold was chosen when the census was first written and is not derived
   from anything. Nothing else in the row turns on it — no other cell has a fragment between
   25 and 244 residues — but a reader is entitled to know the sentence has a dial in it.

2. ON GroEL AND 1GPB, MULTI-CHAIN IS PERFECTLY CONFOUNDED WITH SIZE. Every multi-chain cell on
   those two targets is also >= 768 residues, because their chains are ~524 and 823 long. So
   the twelve zeros are equally consistent with "big fails". What breaks that is FAS, the only
   target with single-chain cells above 823: it reads 0 % at 1024 on two windows and 50 % at
   1536. Size alone does not order the single-chain column either.

3. SINGLE-CHAIN IS NOT SUFFICIENT. Four single-chain cells are at 0 %, and two of them are
   512-residue crops of 1GPB. Which window you take matters as much as how many chains, which
   this row measured long before FAS existed: three 512 windows of one 1GPB chain read
   4.631 / 16.021 / 17.300 A.

WHAT TO SAY, THEN. Multi-chain looks sufficient for failure and single-chain is necessary but
nowhere near sufficient. That is a weaker and more useful claim than "chain count is the
carrier" on its own, and it is what `docs/design-throughput.md` already tells a user: crop to
one chain if you can, keep the extent near 512, and try more than one window.
