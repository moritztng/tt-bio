Two targets, seven points, every residual inside 11%. The two exponents bracket 1.7-1.9, so a
user estimating a size the row has not measured is within about 10% using either.

WHY IT IS WORTH WRITING DOWN. The naive expectation for an attention-shaped model is the
square of the token count, and the card comes in clearly under it: 592 -> 1616 tokens is 2.73x
the tokens and 6.43x the wall, not 7.45x. Per-step dispatch and fixed setup amortise as the
problem grows, and the grid keeps more of the work parallel.

AND IT DOES NOT TRANSFER TO THE CPU REFERENCE. The same algorithm on qb2's CPU draws is
SUPER-quadratic — draw a's 231,908 CPU-s implies an exponent of at least 2.012 at the most
generous occupancy and at least 2.7 at the occupancies actually observed
(`results/upstream_pair_ceiling_refuted.txt`). Fitting on the card and predicting the
reference is what produced a cost band with a measured floor and an imaginary ceiling. Use this
fit for card-side planning only.
