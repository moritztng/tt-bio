# BoltzGen designability at 512 and 1536, n=8 per target, full pipeline.
#
# Two targets per size, not one. The ceiling row's 4.05 A at 512 against 11.48 A at 1536 is
# n=1 on two DIFFERENT crops of big_1831 (A1-512 against A1-1008+B1-528), so it confounds
# size with target identity. Three 512 crops and two 1536 crops give the fixed-size,
# different-target spread that a size effect has to beat.
#
# --budget 8 keeps every generated design in the final set; the scRMSD column comes from the
# analysis step, which runs before filtering either way.
--model boltzgen --target-res 1536 --crop-offset 0    --designs 8 --budget 8 --score-dsg --host-threads 2 --timeout 32400 --tag q
--model boltzgen --target-res 1536 --crop-offset 295  --designs 8 --budget 8 --score-dsg --host-threads 2 --timeout 32400 --tag q
--model boltzgen --target-res 512  --crop-offset 0    --designs 8 --budget 8 --score-dsg --host-threads 2 --timeout 14400 --tag q
--model boltzgen --target-res 512  --crop-offset 496  --designs 8 --budget 8 --score-dsg --host-threads 2 --timeout 14400 --tag q
--model boltzgen --target-res 512  --crop-offset 1319 --designs 8 --budget 8 --score-dsg --host-threads 2 --timeout 14400 --tag q
