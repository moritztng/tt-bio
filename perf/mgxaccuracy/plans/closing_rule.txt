## 2. WHAT COUNTS AS REPLICATION, AND THE THRESHOLD'S ARITHMETIC

GroEL's crops are 0 and 560, not 0 and 100, so its cells are NOT comparable to 1GPB's one by
one. Only the STRUCTURE is comparable: the sign of the interaction and whether the two simple
effects of each factor oppose. Read the sign, never the magnitude, against 1GPB.

Threshold. An n=8 median in this row repeats to 0.1-1.8 A (two cells; the card-2 repeat lands
a third reading ~08:00Z and REPLACES the 1.8 if it is worse). The interaction is a +/-1
combination of four such medians, so in the worst case the four errors add:

    4 x 1.8 = 7.2 A      conservative, assumes every cell wobbles the wrong way at once
    2 x 1.8 = 3.6 A      RMS, if the four are independent

Use the CONSERVATIVE 7.2 A. A claim this row publishes should survive the pessimistic bound.
If the card-2 repeat moves the 1.8, recompute 4x the new worst case before reading GroEL and
say so; do not keep 7.2 as a number of its own.

  REPLICATES   interaction negative AND |interaction| >= 7.2 A.

                  "there is no size rule to apply" stands as written.
