## The object, and why it is not a lever job

Three censuses on three axes converge on `attn_pair_bias.layer_norm_a` in blocks 44, 4 and 0.
Our device name for the same object is `pre_norm_s`, constructed in `PairformerLayer` OUTSIDE
`AttentionPairBias` (`tt_bio/protenix_weights.py:87-88`); a selector keyed on the module on the
call stack misses it, which `of3t-trunkopclass` measured rather than assumed.

The whole arithmetic of the affine gradient is three steps, and it is
`tt_bio/autograd.py:840` plus `_sum_leading` at :683:

    norm = (x - mean) * rstd                 bf16 elementwise
    prod = g * norm                          bf16 elementwise, one rounding per summand
    dW   = sum_t prod_t                      ttnn.sum, fp32 dest accumulation, bf16 OUTPUT

Every one of those three has already been armed by `of3t-trunkopclass` at exactly this class,
and every arm made the reading WORSE:

    prod_fp32   form the summands in fp32                     -0.0108 %
    sum_fp32    ask ttnn.sum for an fp32 output               -0.0232 %
    xhat_fp32   recompute mean/rstd/norm in fp32              unsound standalone (instrument)
    dxcfg       D8's named lever                              inert at 192 firings
    dx_fp32     the activation-gradient path in fp32         -16.3908 %
    all                                                       -23.6950 %

`prod_fp32` and `sum_fp32` between them cover the only two roundings the summation itself
performs, and both are inert to four decimal places. That is the fact this row starts from: if
neither the summand rounding nor the output rounding moves the reading, the residue is not being
MADE by this op, it is ARRIVING in its operands. So the arm is not a flag. The arm is a
substitution, and the question is which operand carries it.

## The metric, fixed now

At each captured site the row holds four quantities for the same parameter:

    dW_ref      upstream float64's own gradient for that tensor (the reference)
    dW_dev      what the card produced
    dW_f64dev   the affine reduction evaluated in float64 on the DEVICE's own x and g
    dW_mixed    the same reduction in float64 with one operand taken from the reference

and splits the error into what the op generated and what it inherited:

    E_tot = || dW_dev    - dW_ref ||
    E_loc = || dW_dev    - dW_f64dev ||        generated by the op's own arithmetic
    E_inh = || dW_f64dev - dW_ref ||           inherited through x and g

    L = sqrt( sum_k E_loc(k)^2 / sum_k E_tot(k)^2 )     over the 96 layer_norm_a tensors
    H = sqrt( sum_k E_inh(k)^2 / sum_k E_tot(k)^2 )     same denominator

`L` and `H` are not complements: the two error vectors can be oblique, so both are reported and
neither is inferred from the other.

**The residue is named LOCAL if `L >= 0.75`.** **It is named INHERITED if `L <= 0.25` and
`H >= 0.75`.** Anything between is a shared carrier and is reported as a share, not as an
answer. Both thresholds are fixed here, before the first capture exists.

Secondary, reported for every site and never used to decide the clause above: the operand
attribution, in the same mass-weighted form and the same denominator,

    A_x = || dW_f64(x_dev, g_ref) - dW_ref || / E_tot        the forward activation's share
    A_g = || dW_f64(x_ref, g_dev) - dW_ref || / E_tot        the cotangent's share

and the cancellation factor of the reduction itself, per site,

    C = sum_t | g_t xhat_t |  /  | sum_t g_t xhat_t |        per output channel, worst and median

## What I expect, and what would refute it

**Primary — INHERITED, carried by the cotangent.** `L <= 0.25`, `H >= 0.75`, and `A_g` at least
3x `A_x`. The reasoning is the two inert levers above: `prod_fp32` and `sum_fp32` exhaust the
roundings inside `dW = sum_t g_t xhat_t`, so a residue of 7.4461 relative at block 4 cannot be
made there. The cotangent `g` arriving at `pre_norm_s`'s output is the accumulation of three
consumers (the q, k and v projections of `AttentionPairBias` read the normalised single
representation), and `Tensor.add_grad` stores the FIRST contribution in whatever dtype it
arrives in — bf16 — and only promotes to fp32 on the second. A three-term accumulation whose
first term is quantised to 8 mantissa bits is not covered by any lever in the table above,
because every lever in that table is inside the LayerNorm closure and this is outside it.

*Refuted by:* `L >= 0.75`, i.e. the card's own three-step arithmetic carries the residue after
all and the two inert levers were measuring something other than what they name.

**Secondary — the cancellation is real and it is the amplifier, not the defect.** The reference
norm at `pairformer_stack.blocks.4.attn_pair_bias.layer_norm_a.weight` is 0.0626 while upstream's
own bf16 reads 3.4262 relative at cos -0.0214, so the quantity is cancellation-limited for
upstream too. I expect `C` in the hundreds or more, and I expect our ratio over upstream's own
bf16 on the same tensor to stay near the published 2.2341x. A residue that is 2.2341x a floor
that is itself 3.4262 is a 2.2x defect on top of a regime, and the deliverable is the 2.2x.

*Refuted by:* `C` of order 10 or less, which would mean the tiny reference norm is not
cancellation at all and the 7.4461 is ordinary wrong arithmetic that should have shown up in
`prod_fp32`.

**Third — pad rows contribute exactly nothing.** `of3t-trunkact` found the cotangent has 0
non-zero pad elements. 85.4 % of the 384-row reduction is therefore structurally zero and the
whole residue lives in 56 rows. I will check this on the captured `g` rather than inherit it,
because it decides whether the reduction length that matters is 384 or 56.

*Refuted by:* any non-zero pad element in a captured `g`, which would make the padded width part
of the mechanism and would also contradict a published finding.
