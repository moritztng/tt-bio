Two thirds of failures never find the epitope.

Split every target by what went wrong. Most failures are not near-misses: no sample in 256 ever lands on the right binding site. Sampling refines poses far faster than it discovers sites.

We tried to beat it. Nothing did.

Six alternative selectors, fixed in advance, each built only from numbers a model already returns — so you could use any of them today, with no ground truth. Every one is level with the shipped selector or worse. The bar was set before running them: beat the baseline with an interval excluding zero on at least two of the four models. Nothing came close.

Structural consensus — ranking samples by agreement with the pool's modal pose — needs the structures themselves and is not tested here.

Spend compute on different models, not on more samples.

Same card-hours per target, split four ways instead of poured into one model. Drag the budget: from a twelfth of a card-hour upward the four-way split beats every single model, and it beats the best of them running 31× longer.

What this is

Four architecturally independent predictors — Boltz-2, OpenDDE-abag, Protenix-v2 and ESMFold2 — each sampled to 256 structures on all 161 scorable targets of the AbAg-XM panel, every structure scored with DockQ against the experimental reference. 383,000 labelled samples on one panel, folded on Tenstorrent Wormhole Galaxy hardware.

Curves are exact, not simulated: the probability that a given sample is the top-ranked member of a random k-subset has a closed form, so every point is computed rather than drawn. Intervals are a 20,000-resample paired bootstrap over targets.

Prior work

That deep sampling raises the ceiling faster than confidence ranking delivers is already published for antibody–antigen complexes, and is not claimed as new here. What this panel adds is the gap expressed as an effective N, the split of ranking failure into within-target and across-target, the finding that most failures are epitope-level, the price of model diversity against sampling depth at measured compute, and a pre-declared null on fixing selection with the scores already available. The findings document names the prior work directly.

Limitations

  • 161 of 164 targets. Three are three-way hetero-hexamers the DockQ scorer does not support; per-model counts after dropping short pools are 153–160.
  • N = 256 is a decision cap, not a measured knee. Three of four models were still gaining at the cap. Nothing here says sampling saturates.
  • Epitope and CDR labels are partial for Protenix-v2 and ESMFold2; those two are analysed at reduced depth and never quoted at 256.
  • Four specific models at specific settings. ESMFold2 runs single-sequence; OpenDDE-abag is antibody-specialised, so its lead is not a general co-folding result.
  • This measures each model's own shipped confidence, not published rankers (ipSAE, pDockQ2, AntiConf, DeepRank-Ab), which need PAE matrices this data does not carry. Whether a learned ranker closes the gap is untested here.
  • Forecasts beyond 256 samples are extrapolations under a no-ceiling fit, so they are optimistic.
