What TT-Bio runs

Structure and binding affinity prediction

--modelFoldsAffinityMSA
boltz2protein, DNA, RNA, ligandyeson by default
protenix-v2protein, DNA, RNA, ligandnoon by default
openfold3protein, RNA, DNAnoon by default
esmfold2single protein chainnonot needed
openddeantibody-antigen complexnoon by default

Protenix-v2 and OpenFold3 are AlphaFold3-family. OpenDDE runs the Protenix-v2 stack plus a structural-token expander. esmfold2-fast is lighter, opendde-abag is the antibody-antigen checkpoint.
